### Related Work 2.1
Use [6]-[9], [12]-[17]. The strongest recent comparison for the sentence about independent versus cooperative control is [13].

### Related Work 2.2
Use foundational counterfactual credit assignment [4], traffic-specific counterfactual actor-critic [11], Shapley reward [18], and Shapley congestion attribution [19]. Explicitly distinguish these training/attribution uses from the present post-training complete characteristic-function analysis.

### Related Work 2.3
Use [20] for safety-enhanced residual RL. Avoid claiming SafeLight uses the same residual action structure as this study; cite it only for the broader residual/safety-preserving principle.

### Related Work 2.4 and Methodology
Use [21] for Shapley value, [22] for core/balanced-game background, [23]-[24] for nucleolus theory, and [25]-[26] for transportation applications of fair/stable cooperative allocation.

### Experimental Setup
Use [1]-[3]. The official LuST repository additionally states that the scenario was generated with SUMO 0.26 and validated with real data and warns that mobility produced with newer SUMO versions cannot automatically be regarded as validated. This supports retaining a pinned legacy-compatible simulation stack.

## Claims that should remain conservative

1. Do not write “the first study” unless a systematic review establishes priority. Use “has received limited attention” or “we are not aware of prior work combining...”.
2. Do not cite Shapley-reward studies as evidence of coalition stability; they support contribution-aware training, not core/nucleolus stability.
3. Do not describe the nucleolus allocation as monetary compensation. In this paper it is a normalized performance-credit/participation-value allocation.
4. Do not describe held-out panel winners as global optima because only seed 9001 enumerates all 512 coalitions.
5. Do not claim the newer 2026 Shapley-attribution paper independently establishes the novelty of this work; use it as evidence that Shapley-based selective training/attribution is an active research direction.
